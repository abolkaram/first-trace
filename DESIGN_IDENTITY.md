# Identity brief

- Mechanism signature: earliest semantic attestation over an ordered, digest-bound timeline.
- Product metaphor: archaeological core and field notebook.
- Narrative voice: observational, chronological, short field annotations.
- Visual grammar: vertical strata, mineral colors, narrow specimen labels, depth ruler.
- Interaction grammar: pin one reel, descend through layers, lift the first positive trace.
- Result presentation: a highlighted layer and complete presence vector, never a dashboard.
- Evidence story: every snapshot is refetched and digest-checked; fixtures are disclosed demonstrations.
